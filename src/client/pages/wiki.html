Getting Started

Welcome to iXat! This guide will help you get up and running with all the features our platform has to offer.

Creating Your Account

To get started, you'll need to create an account:

  • Click the "Register" button in the top navigation
  • Choose a unique username (3-20 characters)
  • Enter a valid email address
  • Create a secure password (minimum 6 characters)
  • Optionally set a display nickname

Your First Login

After registration, you can log in using your username or email address. Once logged in, you'll have access to:

💬 Chat

Join conversations in real-time

🛒 Store

Purchase powers and items

👤 Profile

Customize your appearance

⚡ Powers

Use special abilities

Starting Balance

New users receive 1,000 xats to get started. You can use these to purchase powers and items from the store.

Chat Features

iXat offers a rich chat experience with many features to enhance your conversations.

Basic Chatting

To send a message, simply type in the message box and press Enter or click Send. Your messages will appear in the chat with your username and avatar.

Message Formatting

You can format your messages using special codes:

/bold text/ - Makes text bold *italic text* - Makes text italic _underlined text_ - Underlines text ~strikethrough text~ - Strikes through text

Private Messages

To send a private message to another user, click on their name in the user list and select "Private Message". Private conversations open in a separate tab.

Emojis and Smilies

Express yourself with emojis and smilies. Type :smile: for 😊 or use the emoji picker in the chat interface.

Powers

Powers are special abilities that enhance your chat experience. They can be purchased from the store or earned through various activities.

Types of Powers

  • Visual Powers: Change how your messages appear
  • Functional Powers: Add special abilities to your account
  • Decorative Powers: Add visual effects and animations
  • Utility Powers: Help with moderation or management

Using Powers

Once you own a power, you can activate it from your profile or the powers page. Some powers are automatic, while others require manual activation.

Power Management

You can view all your owned powers, check their status, and manage their settings from the Powers section of your profile.

Store

The iXat store is where you can purchase powers, packages, and other items to enhance your experience.

Purchasing Items

To buy items from the store:

  1. Navigate to the Store page
  2. Browse available items
  3. Click "Buy" on the item you want
  4. Confirm your purchase

Payment Methods

Currently, all purchases are made using xats (our virtual currency). You can earn xats through various activities or purchase xat packages.

Packages

Xat packages offer great value for money, providing more xats than purchasing them individually. Check the Packages tab in the store for current offers.

Profile & Settings

Customize your iXat experience by managing your profile and account settings.

Profile Information

Update your profile with:

  • Display nickname
  • Avatar selection
  • Personal description
  • Contact information
  • Privacy settings

Account Settings

Manage your account preferences including:

  • Password changes
  • Email notifications
  • Privacy controls
  • Language preferences

Moderation

iXat has a comprehensive moderation system to ensure a safe and enjoyable environment for all users.

Reporting Users

If you encounter inappropriate behavior, you can report users by clicking on their name and selecting "Report". Our moderation team will review all reports.

Moderation Actions

Moderators and administrators can take various actions including:

  • Warnings
  • Temporary bans
  • Permanent bans
  • Message deletion

Appeals Process

If you believe you've been unfairly moderated, you can appeal the decision through our support system.

Troubleshooting

Having issues? Here are some common solutions to help you get back on track.

Login Problems

If you can't log in:

  • Check your username/email and password
  • Try resetting your password
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies
  • Try a different browser

Chat Issues

If chat isn't working properly:

  • Refresh the page
  • Check your internet connection
  • Disable browser extensions temporarily
  • Try incognito/private browsing mode

Getting Help

If you need additional assistance:

  • Visit the Forum for community support
  • Check our FAQ section
  • Contact our support team
